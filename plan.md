# Professional Tenant Onboarding Plan

This plan details the steps following a successful contract signature and payment.

## Phase 1: Post-Payment Initialization
- **Auto-Provisioning**: The system detects the `PAID` status and initializes an Onboarding Record.
- **Welcome Kit**: Tenant receives an automated "Welcome Home" portal access.

## Phase 2: Digital Move-In Inspection (Condition Report)
- **Visual Evidence**: Owner uploads timestamped photos of every room.
- **Tenant Validation**: Tenant reviews photos and can upload counter-photos of any existing damage (scratches, leaks, etc.).
- **Digital Sign-off**: Both parties sign the condition report to avoid future security deposit disputes.

## Phase 3: Utility & Service Transition
- **Utility Checklist**: Automated reminders for Power, Water, and Gas transfers.
- **Service Registration**: Tracking the status of Internet/WiFi setup.
- **Emergency Contacts**: Providing a list of building managers and emergency services.

## Phase 4: Orientation & Handover
- **Virtual Tour/Guide**: Owner provides a guide for appliance usage, trash schedules, and parking rules.
- **Key Handover Acknowledgement**: Digital confirmation when keys/access cards are physically received.

## Phase 5: PG & Hostel Specific Onboarding (Co-living POV)
- **Roommate Introduction**: If in a shared room, a brief "ice-breaker" or basic profile check (if privacy settings allow).
- **Inventory Handover**: Detailed checklist for furnished items (Bed, Mattress, Study Table, Locker with keys).
- **Utility & Mess Guide**: Clear timings for meals (Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner) and laundry room access.
- **Rules & Regulations**: Curfew timings, guest policy (night-stay rules), and noise level policies.
- **Caretaker Contact**: Direct link to the on-site warden or caretaker for immediate facility issues.

## Phase 6: Post-Move-In Feedback
- **Settling-in Survey**: 7 days after move-in, a quick check-up on any immediate issues.
- **Review Loop**: Finalizing the onboarding phase and moving to active tenancy management.
